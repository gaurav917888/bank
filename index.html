Important differences for preparation

A compact planning checklist.

Clerk track

  • SBI Clerk: state-wise recruitment; LPT after Mains where applicable; no interview.
  • IBPS Clerk: CSA recruitment across participating public-sector banks; 2026 Mains is the revised 155Q / 200 marks / 120 min paper.
  • Both prelims have English + Quant/Numerical + Reasoning with 20 minutes per section.

RRB track

  • RRB PO: Officer Scale-I; Prelims + Mains + Interview.
  • RRB Office Assistant: Prelims + Mains; no interview; final merit is based on Mains.
  • RRB 2026 UP vacancy remains 425 Scale-I and 1,200 Office Assistant in total after the latest September update.
