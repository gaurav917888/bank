How to read the vacancy numbers

For SBI, the table uses regular GEN seats in Uttar Pradesh; backlog seats are separate. For IBPS and RRB, vacancy tables are indicative and can be revised based on participating-bank reporting. Therefore, use 2026 figures as the latest published position, not as a guarantee of final allotment numbers.

Important differences for preparation

A compact planning checklist.

Clerk track

  • SBI Clerk: state-wise recruitment; LPT after Mains where applicable.
  • IBPS Clerk: CSA recruitment across participating public-sector banks; Mains is the decisive written stage.
  • Both have 100-mark Prelims with English + Quant + Reasoning.

RRB track

  • RRB PO: Officer Scale-I; Prelims + Mains + Interview.
  • RRB Office Assistant: Prelims + Mains; no interview.
  • RRB Prelims is only 80 marks, so the historical cutoff is naturally on a different scale from clerk prelims.
